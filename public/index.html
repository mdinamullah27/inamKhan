About Me

Bridging applied AI engineering and published research

I'm an AI/ML Engineer and Computer Science graduate from Daffodil International University (CGPA 3.63/4.00), with experience spanning academic research and production AI development. My research explored lightweight transformer architectures (CCT) for medical image analysis — lung disease classification, diabetic retinopathy diagnosis, and explainable AI — resulting in 7 publications in Q1 journals including IEEE Access, MDPI Biomedicines, and Neural Computing and Applications.

Currently at Ethics Advance Technology Ltd, I develop LLM-powered systems: integrating domain-adapted LLMs with prompt engineering, document chunking, embeddings, vector databases and RAG pipelines, and deploying them into production via FastAPI REST endpoints and web application integration. Previously, as a Research Assistant at United International University, I designed Compact Convolutional Transformer architectures and conducted medical AI research.
